Ghost list is a list of all ghosts
The fastest ghosts are the currently loaded ones
The ghosts are in the same order (based on ID) as the VehicleState visibilities

If you restart on the first run (before you complete a run) a ghost appears in MLFeed but two appear in VehicleState
Once you complete your first run, only one ghost appears in VehicleState but two in MLFeed

After adding a ghost and restarting, VehicleState IDs and MLFeed IDs are in the same order

If you add a ghost after only partially finishing a run, the first two entries (in sorted order) are the personal best entries
If there are not the same number of ghosts, there is no way to determine who is who reliably

Final statement:
Ghosts IDs and VehicleState Entity IDs are always in the same order as each other
They aren't necessarily always in sorted order (when recieving as an array)
